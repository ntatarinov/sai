## Что не так

The page code is right by reading and by the validator's probes. What is missing are tests: the third test-design gate of REQ-057 found 10 survivors of the validator's own mutants that no test kills. F1 (Medium, "Start over" on the screen of the result) was closed at step 24 by the owner's one-time allowance (`decisions/0081`); F2-F11 were Low, did not block, and were outside that allowance. Every item below was confirmed by a probe of the validator that passes on the real code and fails on the mutant (except 9, 10 and 11, see there). The files are `tools/src/picker/public/*.js` (the code) and `tools/test/picker-page*.js` (the tests); line numbers are from step 23 and moved a little after step 24.

1. **F2, mutant R09** (`app.js:454`, `setHeadNet(ui.head, null)` in `showResult`): after a failed change of language (a block under the header) the visitor presses "Show the result", the net is back, the result opens and the old block stays under the header; its button reads `state.net.lang`, but `state.net` is null there (a click would throw). `QP-7f` has "failed change, then failed result" but not "failed change, then good result".
2. **F3, mutant R07** (`app.js:468`, `toTop()` in `showResult`): no scroll to the top when the result opens; `QP-5a` pins scroll for the moves between questions, not for the result (the focus on the result heading is pinned).
3. **F4, mutants C02 and C08** (`app.js:117` `hasSaved`, `:293` `onStart`): the card is shown with saved answers, then the storage starts to refuse writes, the visitor presses a language button; the card must come back without "Continue", and "Take the survey" must start with no answers. `QP-4c` checks only a storage that refuses from the start.
4. **F5, mutant N15** (`app.js:556`, `setNetBlock(ui.question, null)` in `langFailed`): a failed "Show the result" followed by a failed change of language leaves two blocks and two elements with `id="net-text"`; `QP-7f` has only the opposite order.
5. **F6, mutant W07** (`app.js:101`, `wipe`): the refusal of `removeItem` alone is ignored; `QP-4c` makes all three storage calls refuse, so `wipe()` returns at once. The helper option `failRemove` exists and is not used alone.
6. **F7, mutant S08** (`store.js:13`, `VERSION_FORM` without `^` and `$`): a stored version of 65 signs (the right one plus "0") must be no version; with the mutant the card shows "the questionnaire changed". Rows missing in `QM-3a` and a page case in `QP-4e`.
7. **F8, mutant K09** (`screens.js:294`): the slot of the net block put before the form; `QP-7b` finds the block but does not compare its place with the form, and `QP-5c` (order in the tree is the order of the tabs) does not include the block.
8. **F9, mutant L02** (`app.js:160`, `announce(t('quiz_loading'))`): no test reads the live region while the loading screen is open.
9. **F10, mutants R01-R04 and N30** (`app.js:456-466`, the call of `renderResult`; design 4.9): the hand-off to the result screen (`ctx.result`, `ctx.answers`, `ctx.questionnaire`, `ctx.lang`, `ctx.name`) is not pinned, because the temporary screen of the result ignores these fields; no probe is possible through the page as it is. **To be taken by REQ-058**, which replaces `result.js` as a whole.
10. **F11 = K2 of step 21, mutants A01-A07, A09, A10** (`screens.js:149-215` and `:248-290`, `dom.js:8-22`): no page test walks the attributes of the tree, so tenant text can reach an attribute (`title`, `aria-label`, `placeholder`, `style`, `href`) without a red test. `QS-3` forbids only the literal `setAttribute('style'` and `.style`, so a `style` attribute made through the `attrs` object breaks design rule D11 unseen (the CSP would refuse it in the browser). The code is safe by reading: every attribute name is a constant. The earlier mutant D of step 21 (`onmouseover` from every string child) is now killed by `QM-6a`.
11. **N2 of step 15** (`tools/test/picker-page.test.js`, `assertLeftAlone`, around 2475-2515): the design says the focus is not taken back by the dropped change of language; `QP-9j` checks the focus on the same-version path, `QP-9k` and `QP-9l` do not check it after the late answer. Safe by position of the guard, not pinned.
12. **L3 of step 22** (`QP-2e`, `picker-page.test.js`, around 612-810): one test holds eight independent scenarios, some with default assertion messages, so a failure at line N does not say which scenario failed. Not a defect of the check.
13. **L1 of step 22** (`tools/test/picker-page-dom.js`, `hide()` and the `Node` constructor): after `hide()` every field except `tag` is non-enumerable, so `assert.deepEqual` of two different nodes compares only the tag and passes for any two nodes of one tag; no test does this now (checked by script), but the comment of `hide()` does not name the trap (see AP-124).
