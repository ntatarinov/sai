# Release report ISS-0103-20261103-01 - the flaky test E2K-18 that turned `main` red (defect ISS-0103, requirement REQ-104)

Report date: 2026-11-03 (UTC, from the clock). Process: WF-03 (defect) for ISS-0103; it belongs to the requirement REQ-104
(the real-Chrome end-to-end kit), which was released in the run `E2E104-20261103-01` (`docs/releases/E2E104-20261103-01.md`).
The push to `main` is an ordinary push under `decisions/0048`, `0062`, `0079` item 1, after the PASS of RELEASE-VALIDATOR
(`handoffs/ISS-0103-20261103-01/step-09-release-validator.json`, completed 2026-11-03T19:33:43Z) and after the bookkeeping of
DOC-UPDATER (`step-10-doc-updater.json`); the route of the handoff of step 11 says that ORCH checked its files and fields.
The report is in English by `decisions/0080`, item 2.

**In short.** The first push of REQ-104 (commit `30ab39c`) turned `check.yml` on `main` red: one test, E2K-18, failed on the
GitHub runner (run 41685488320). The kit code was not at fault: the test looked at a killed stand-in process once, right
after the kill, and a killed process is not gone at that same moment. The fix is in tests only: the test now waits, with a
limit of 5 seconds, until the process and its group are really gone. `main` stayed red from that push (17:33:54Z) until the
green run of this run's push (19:56:26Z), about 2 h 22 min (see "Commits and GitHub runs").

## What was done

- **Diagnosis** (`step-01-issue-fixer.json`): the cause is a single check of the death of a killed process, with no wait
  (a race against the death and the reaping of the process). Three places of `tools/test/e2e-kit.test.js` were exposed: the
  assertion of E2K-18 and the two loops of E2K-38. The kit code (`tools/test/e2e/kit/`) did not change.
- **Fix** (tests only, `tools/test/e2e-kit.test.js`): a helper `waitGone` (pid and group give ESRCH, limit 5000 ms) used in the
  three places; two new tests: E2K-62 (a deterministic regression: a killed, not reaped process stays in state Z, the old
  single check is false, the new wait ends true after the release) and E2K-63 (a guard of the call sites). The count of tests
  is now 2187 (was 2185).
- **Documents:** `decisions/0108` (addendum: a test that has seen a process end by a kill waits with a limit for ESRCH of the
  pid and its group), `docs/design/REQ-104-e2e-chrome-kit.md` (fact F20 corrected, rows E2K-18 and E2K-38 changed, rows
  E2K-62 and E2K-63 added), `docs/anti-patterns.md` (AP-144: a timing fact taken on one machine was taken for a fact of all
  machines), `STATE.md`, `PLAN.md`.
- **Defects:** ISS-0103 is closed (2026-11-03) with the real outcome and the limits of the proof; ISS-0104 (Low, open) is new:
  the known limits of the guard E2K-63 and two surviving mutants (M20, M21) of E2K-62.
