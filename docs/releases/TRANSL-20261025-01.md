## What was fixed after the gate (pass 1 findings and the rework)

- **Literals that tools read.** «неизвестно» in `handoffs.md` and «FAIL принят» in `orchestrator.md` and `handoffs.md` were
  translated by mistake; they are Russian again. The tool still accepts the time word (`isUnknownTime` checked on both forms).
- **Cyrillic identifiers.** Letters that were turned into Latin (`6а`-`6в`, `3а`, `Д7`, `Ф7`, `WEB-15 (б)` and others) are
  Cyrillic again; a record-by-record comparison with the tag shows no identifier lost.
- **FS-16 and the other guard tests.** The guard test FS-16 was blind to the English form of the old facts; it now knows the
  Russian and the English forms, and mutants turn it red.
- **21 requirement micro-amendments** in `docs/requirements.yaml` (REQ-001, 003, 005, 006, 008, 009, 010, 011, 013, 014, 035,
  036, 038, 039, 042, 047, 050, 051, 052, 053, 068): only a quoted heading or phrase of a translated file was changed to the
  English one that now exists. Status, `depends_on`, owner, title and the number of criteria are equal in all 68 blocks; each
  of the 21 blocks has one event line in its status history (WF-01, micro-amendment).
- **Wording.** One wording for each standard heading in the 37 role files; calques ("foreign", "at the order of") replaced;
  placeholders in commands made English (`<outside the project>`, `<line>`); a few Low findings fixed in step 10.
