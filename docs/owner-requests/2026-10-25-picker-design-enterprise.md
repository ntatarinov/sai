# Owner request 2026-10-25: the picker site must look strong for 1C and SAP users

Owner's words, verbatim (session message after the release of REQ-057):

```text
опрос и сайт очень слабый и слабый. надо дизайн сделать не такойк как ты принмаешь то есть не базовый, так чтобы 1с и sap пользователи оценили сайт
```

How ORCH understood it (the owner may correct): the questionnaire page (card, questions, result) looks like a basic template. The visitors are small and medium businesses that work in 1C and SAP; they must feel a serious, trustworthy, professional product at first sight. First a clickable mockup outside the project for the owner to judge the style; only after the owner accepts a direction does it become a requirement (WF-01) and code. The tenant data, the engine, the server and the tests of REQ-057 stay as they are; the redesign touches only how it looks and how the screens are laid out.
