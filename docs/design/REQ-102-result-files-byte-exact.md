## 0. Summary

1. **One new pure function `findSecrets(text)`** in `tools/src/pixel-office/mask.js` (section 2): returns `null` or `{ form }` with `form` one of `F1…F9` (the forms Ф1–Ф9 of the requirement); no value, no offset. Linear time, also for a line that holds thousands of unclosed groups (G1: a per-pass record of the failed closer searches, section 2.2 item 5; measured F22) and for a value that is a long run of closing brackets (V1: the closers at the end of a value are dropped by one counting pass and one walk back, never by a search per dropped closer, section 2.2 item 3; measured F33). No rule of `maskSecrets` is edited (HK-21 stays green); no regular expression with a repeated group is used on unbounded text (a stack overflow was measured, fact F11).
2. **Three inputs, one protection.** `#publish` in `store.js` (the common point of `writeResultFile`, `importWorkFile`, `importResultBytes`) calls `findSecrets` on the content and throws `TeamError("E_WORK_FILE")` with `details.reason = "secret"` before anything is prepared or written (section 3.1). The content is stored as given; the sha256 is the hash of the original.
3. **BOM.** Two decoders of result bytes get `ignoreBOM: true` (`files.js` `decoder`, a new `resultUtf8` in `store.js`); the decoder `strictUtf8` that parses scenario bytes is **not** changed (it would turn a scenario with a BOM into a parse error; fact F5).
4. **`runner.js`.** `acceptResult` step 1 (claude-cli path) reads **all** files, scans each, and on any hit removes the sources of all hit files from `work/` and fails the attempt with the new key `FAIL_DETAIL.secret`; the visible text names the refused files (names only, G7), and only those whose name the journal rules leave unchanged (N1, `decisions/0102` item 28; section 3.2); the event detail, the team's question and the error line of `run-task` carry the same text. `depsOf` no longer masks: it scans, and a file with a hit is **withheld** (constant text, no exception) — the decision for finding F4 of the gate (section 3.3).
5. **`language.js`:** `FAIL_DETAIL.secret`, the function `secretDetail(names)` that adds the names of the refused files to it (G7) and decides which names may be shown (N1), a conditional `PHRASES` row for `TASK_FAILED` + `result_file` + a detail that starts with `FAIL_DETAIL.secret`, the constant `FILE_WITHHELD`. `engine/single-run.js`, `executors/*`, `engine/*` are not changed (section 3.4).
6. **Sketch result.** The whole design was applied to a fresh clone (`<scratch>/cd/work`): the 49 team/exec test files turn **12** tests red, not 8 — the eight of the table **and** SF-10, SF-11, SF-12, SF-15 (facts F9, F10). ### 2.1 `findSecrets(text)` — `tools/src/pixel-office/mask.js` (new export)

- **Signature:** `findSecrets(text: string): null | { form: "F1" | "F2" | "F3" | "F4" | "F5" | "F6" | "F7" | "F8" | "F9" }`. - **Names:** the new code does not redeclare a name that `mask.js` already has (`TOKEN_CHAR`, `SCHEME_CHAR`, `SPACE`, `isSpace`, `isTokenChar`, `isLineEnd`, `QUOTES`, `scanQuoted`, `NAMES`, `NAME_TAIL`, `SEGMENT`, `endsWithName`, `RULES`): it reuses `SCHEME_CHAR`, `QUOTES`, `isLineEnd` as they are, and gives its own functions a distinct prefix (the sketch clash on `isLineEnd`, `QUOTES`, `scanQuoted` and four more names stopped the module from loading until renamed; the old `scanQuoted` returns no content, so the new quoted reader is a new function).

### 2.2 The rules, as steps

**Value reader** `readValue(text, i, allowBareId)` → `{ end, kind }`, `kind` ∈ `none` (empty value: the end of the line, the end of the text, or a pair of quotes in a row), `keep`, `literal`. Used by Ф2 (without scheme, and after a scheme with `allowBareId = false`), Ф8 (`allowBareId = false`) and Ф9 (`allowBareId = true`).

1. At `i` a `LINE_END` or the end of text → `none`.
2. At `i` a `QUOTE` → a quoted value: read to the same quote on the same line (a backslash escapes the next character unless that is a `LINE_END`); without a closing quote to the end of the line or text. Empty content → `none`. Content that is a placeholder (З) or a template (Ш) as a **whole** → `keep`; everything else → `literal`. The classes В1–В4 are **not** applied to a quoted value (`'data.token'` is a literal).
3. Otherwise an unquoted value: if a template group starts at `i` (`${{…}}` to `}}`, `${…}` to the first `}`, `{{…}}` to `}}`, `$(…)` to the first `)`, `<…>` to the first `>` with no `<` inside; non-empty; **one line**) the group is taken whole. **Every opener has exactly one closer and there is no fallback from one group form to another (N4):** `${{` is closed only by `}}`, so `${{ a }`, which a single `}` closes, has no `}}`, the `${{` stays unclosed and is a `literal` (it is not read as a `${…}` group closed by that `}`); `<…>` is cut at a second `<` (the search fails, the `<` stays unclosed and is a `literal`: `<a<b>`). From there (or from `i`) the value runs to the first `WS` character, `QUOTE`, `,` or `;`. **The closers at the end of the value are dropped in one pass (V1).** A trailing `)`, `]` or `}` is dropped when the value holds more closers of its kind than openers of its kind (`(`, `[`, `{`): the pairing reading of the requirement («у которых в самом значении нет парной открывающей»); `{ token: data.token}` → `data.token`, `f(a)` keeps its `)`, `f(a))` loses the second one. The step has no search and has two parts: (a) six counters, `open[k]` and `close[k]` for the three kinds, are filled in **one forward pass** over the raw value (the pass that finds the end of the value, or one pass over the text already cut; each character is looked at once); (b) **one walk back** from the end of the value: while the last character that is left is a closer whose `close[k] > open[k]`, drop it and take one off `close[k]`; stop at the first character that is not such a closer. A step of the walk costs O(1), so the whole step costs O(length of the value). **Forbidden:** looking for an opener (an `indexOf`, a loop, a count, a `split`) in the rest of the value for each dropped closer. Each opener kind is looked up once per value, never re-scanned per dropped closer. The plain reading of the earlier wording («have no opener of their own kind») does exactly that: it gives the oracle of section 18 and passes all eleven earlier families, and it is quadratic on `token=` followed by a long run of closers (F33: 17.1 s at 1 MiB on the measuring machine; the validator measured 8.6 s at 1 MiB and 137.9 s at 4 MiB on its own). Family 12 (section 10) and the mutant m43 guard this step. An empty remainder → `none`.
4. Classes, in this order, any of which gives `keep`: З placeholder (the whole value, case-insensitive: one of the 26 words; or ≥ 3 characters of only `*`, `x`, `X`; or starts with `your`; or contains `example`); Ш template (the whole value is one group as in step 3, or `$NAME`, or `%NAME%` with `NAME` = `[A-Za-z_][A-Za-z0-9_]*`); В1 path (two or more identifiers joined by `.` or `?.`; identifier = Latin letters, `_`, `$`, no digits); В2 call (an identifier or path directly followed by `(`); В3 bare identifier — **only when `allowBareId`** — (letters and `_` only, and one of: a word of the list of types and keywords, case-insensitive; contains `_` and a letter; has a lower-case letter directly before an upper-case one); В4 only characters of `{ [ ( | >`. Anything else non-empty → `literal`.
5. **The group search is one forward scan from the group start** that stops at the closer, at a `LINE_END` or at the end of the text (for `<…>` also at a second `<`). A text-wide `indexOf` for the closer or for the line end is forbidden (F12). **A failed search is remembered (G1).** A failed search is not an error: the text is then read as an ordinary value, by the classes of step 4. An unclosed `${`, `${{` or `<` is then a `literal` and ends the scan with a hit; an unclosed `{{` is the kept class В4 and an unclosed `$(` the kept class В2 (an identifier `$` followed by `(`), so for these two the pass goes on, and the next name or `Bearer` of the same line starts the same failed search again. Without a record every such search would read to the end of the line: quadratic (measured, F22: `token={{ ` repeated to 256 KiB with no line end costs 10 s, and 4 times the input costs 16 times the time). (The earlier wording of this item assumed that the fallback always ends the scan; that holds only for the classes that are literals.) The rule:
   - Each pass of `findSecrets` that reads values (the pass of F8 and the pass of F2/F9; F3 reads no group) holds a record `failed[closer]` for the three closers `}}` (used by `${{` and by `{{`), `}` (by `${`) and `)` (by `$(`). It starts empty and is local to the pass; nothing is kept between calls.
   - A search for a closer from the position `s` (the first character after the opener) first reads the record: if `from ≤ s ≤ lineEnd` it fails at once, without a scan (no closer lies between `from` and `lineEnd`, and `s` lies inside). Otherwise it scans. A scan that ends at a `LINE_END` or at the end of the text writes `{ from: s, lineEnd: <that position> }` into the record. A scan that ends at the closer, at an empty group (the closer stands at `s`), or at the second `<` of an `<…>` group writes nothing: the scans of `<…>` groups never overlap, because the next one starts at that second `<`.
   - The record never misses: a pass is monotone (each scan starts at or after the place where the previous value ended; an empty value moves on by one position at least), so every later search starts at `s' ≥ from`.
   - Cost: a character is read by at most one scan that ends in a closer (the value that took the group moves the pass beyond it), by the one failed scan of each of the three closers, and by one `<…>` scan: five reads at most, so the total time on any line is linear in its length. The checks of step 4 that ask "is the whole value one group" use the end found in step 3 or one pass over the value, which the pass consumes anyway; they start no search of their own.
   - The closer drop of item 3 adds at most two reads of the value (the counters and the walk back) and starts no search, so the total stays linear (F33).
   - The mutant that drops the record (m31, section 11) is caught by family 11 of the instrument (section 10); the mutant that re-scans the value for every dropped closer (m43) by family 12.
6. Each of the helper checks (path, call, identifier, placeholder, template, block opener) is **one forward pass** over the value, never a `RegExp` with a repeated group (F11). **The drop of the closers at the end of the value (item 3) is one pass as well**: one forward pass for the counters and one walk back, never a search per dropped closer (V1).

## 3. The exact order of calls

### 3.1 `store.js`, `#publish({ task, name, content, kind, fromWork })`

The three inputs differ only before this point: `writeResultFile` (a string), `importWorkFile` (`checkWorkPath`, `checkName`, writer open check, `readWorkFile` with the BOM decoder), `importResultBytes` (writer open check, `Buffer` check, 5 MiB check, `resultUtf8.decode`).

Steps of `#publish`, in order (the changed lines are 4–6):

1. writer broken or closed → `writerBroken()` (as before).
2. `checkFreeName(filesDir, name)` — bad name or taken name (as before; these refusals win over the secret refusal).
3. `content` must be a string → else `eventError("content")` (as before).
4. `contentOf(content)` → `{ bytes, sha256 }` of the **original** (replaces `contentOf(masked)`); size over 5 MiB → `eventError("content")` (as before, now before the scan so that a huge string is not scanned).
5. `findSecrets(content)`; on a hit → throw the error of 2.4. **Nothing is prepared, nothing is written.**
6. `#prepare` of the `RESULT_FILE` event, then `publishResultFile(filesDir, name, content)` (the original text, not a masked one), the equality of the two hashes, then `#commit` with the removal of the file when the event cannot be written (as before).

This is the protection in the same operation as the action it protects: the scan, the write and the event are one synchronous call of the writer; no "check now, act later" exists between them. The scan stays here even though `runner.js` scans earlier (3.2): `runner.js` protects only the claude-cli path; `#publish` protects the other inputs and the gap between the read of step 1 and the read of the import.

`maskSecrets` stays imported in `store.js` for the text fields (221, 310, 556 on the tag) — the one-line import becomes `import { findSecrets, maskSecrets } from "../pixel-office/mask.js";` (TM-44 needs a single line ending in `mask.js";`; the line is under 80 characters, so `prettier` keeps it on one line). `runner.js` gets `import { findSecrets, prepareText } from "../pixel-office/mask.js";` (its `maskSecrets` had only the one use).

### 3.2 `runner.js`, `acceptResult(result, attempt)`

Local helpers added inside `runTask`: `secretRefusal(names)` → `text = secretDetail(names)` (all refused names; `secretDetail` filters and counts, section 2.4), then `refusal(executorError("E_TASK_FAILED", text), "secret", text)` (G7: the object returned by `refusal` gets a third field `detailText`, `undefined` for every other refusal); `isSecretRefusal(err)` → a `TeamError` with code `E_WORK_FILE` and `details.reason === "secret"`. `failTask(reason, detailKey, detailText)` takes an optional third argument and writes `detailText ?? FAIL_DETAIL[detailKey]` into `detail`; the call site passes `accepted.detailText`, so the same text goes to the event and to the error of the command (N1: the error of the command is never built from the names by another route; the mutant m39 does exactly that). The reason code of the event stays `result_file` (the enumeration of `TASK_FAILED.reason` in `schema.js` is not changed: the 37-event catalogue stays). Measured in the sketch (F24, F28): one refused file with a one-dot name gives `… найден секрет (файл: t-0001-1-memo_research.md)` in `detail`, in the team's question in `chat` and in the error line; a clean file beside it is not named; five refused files give `(файлы: t-0001-1-a.md, t-0001-1-b.md, t-0001-1-c.md и ещё 2)`; a file with a two-dot name is not named in any of the three (N1).

**claude-cli path, step 1 (before the first write), one full pass** — no early return:

1. For each file: path check (`checkWorkPath`); on failure mark `refused` and go on.
2. `readWorkFile` (now with the BOM decoder); a file refusal (`E_WORK_FILE`, `E_FILE_NAME`, `E_FILE_EXISTS`, `E_EVENT`) marks `refused` and goes on; anything else is thrown as before.
3. `findSecrets(content)`: on a hit add the path to `secretPaths` and go on (the name check is skipped for it, so that a secret is refused as a secret whatever the name). G7, N1: the accepted name `<task>-<attempt>-<last part of the path>` is added to `secretNames` — every hit file, whatever its name (names only; `secretDetail` decides which of them may be shown and counts the rest, section 2.4).
4. The name check (`checkResultName`) as before (mark `refused`), then `planned.push`.
5. After the loop: if `secretPaths` is not empty → `removeSource(workDir, path)` for **each** of them, then return `secretRefusal(secretNames)`. Else if `refused` → return `fileRefusal()` (as before). Else go on to the writes.

Consequences measured in F7: a clean file named beside a secret file is **not** accepted and its source stays in `work/` (as in EX-42); every secret source is gone; one `RUN_STARTED` per task; no retry (`failTask` + `finish("failed")`, as for every `result_file` refusal). `removeSource` is the existing function: it walks the path again, refuses links, and on a failed unlink pushes the existing notice «Не удалось удалить исходный файл результата из рабочей папки» (then the secret stays in a 0700 draft folder and the user is told). A file that is a link or has more than one hard link is refused by `readWorkFile` (reason `link`) before the scan, so the scan never sees it and `removeSource` is never asked to follow a link.

**Writes (loop over `planned`)** — unchanged, with one new catch branch before `isFileRefusal`: `isSecretRefusal(err)` → `removeSource` of this item (when it has a `path`) and `return secretRefusal([item.name])`. In the claude-cli path this branch is reached only if the file changed between step 1 and the import (the process of the agent is already finished); in the scripted path it is the normal route.

**Not claude-cli (scripted) path:** step 1 stays "name check only" (the files are in memory). Files are accepted one by one by `importResultBytes`; the first hit gives `secretRefusal([item.name])` and the files accepted before it **stay** (the written exclusion of the requirement; fact F8 D). The task fails, the process lives.

**Step 4 (remove the sources of accepted files)** is unchanged. A refused attempt never reaches it.

`closeAfterError` keeps `failTask("result_file", "result_file")` for an unexpected error while `accepting` — unchanged.

### 3.3 `runner.js`, `depsOf(writer, task)` — the decision for finding F4 of the gate

The file is read as before (`fs.readFileSync(…, {encoding:"utf8", flag: NOFOLLOW})`; the BOM stays, F4; `E_DISK` on a read error stays). Then:

1. `findSecrets(content)` is `null` → `content` is handed out as read (byte-exact, no masking).
2. A hit → the entry is `{ task, name, content: FILE_WITHHELD }`. **No exception, no event, the file on disk is not touched.**

Why a scan at all, and why "withhold": `depsOf` feeds the prompt that goes to an external model (the work run, the check of a task, the check of the goal); a stored file is normally clean (the same check ran at the write), but `files/` can hold a file the new core would have refused — a file written by the old core (it carries the label: `password=[скрыто]`, `token: [скрыто]`, `Authorization: [скрыто]`, `https://u:[скрыто]@h/x` all hit, F14) or placed by hand. Throwing would stop the whole engine for an old project: `defaultContext(…)` is evaluated outside any `try` in `engine.js` `workRun` (`:458`, the `try` starts after it) and in the reassign run and the plan run (`:591`, `:667`) — a crash path (Q10). Handing the file out would send what the core refuses to store. Withholding keeps the engine running; the reviewer sees the name of the file and the constant text (and the check of the task fails with the reviewer's own notes, the usual way). Cost: an old project whose masked file contains a pair like `password=[скрыто]` loses that file's content for later runs — named; question О3, answered by `decisions/0102` item 25.

All callers of `depsOf` get the same function; none is edited: `defaultContext` (`runner.js:145`), which `runTask` (`runner.js:198`), `review.js:54` and `:71` and `engine.js:458`, `:591`, `:667` call, and the direct calls `review.js:55` and `:72`. `depsOf` is the **one** place that reads the content of `files/` (F10b: nine reads in the team core and the web server, only `runner.js:125` reads `files/`), so a fix in one place of two (R5 of the diagnosis) cannot happen again.

### 3.4 What is not changed, and why

- `engine/single-run.js`: `importOutputs` (a run without a task) calls `writer.importResultBytes` one file at a time; a hit throws the `E_WORK_FILE` error out of the loop, the existing `catch` runs `closeAfterError()` (the run is finished as failed and charged) and returns `{ result: null, run_id, error }` — the process does not fall (F8 G). Files accepted before the hit stay. The written exclusion of the requirement is exactly this behaviour; no helper is shared with `runner.js` (decision 0102 item 5).
- `executors/*`, `engine/*`, `view.js`, `schema.js`: no edit. The reason code `result_file` and the 37 event types stay.
- `maskSecrets` and its ten callers' roles: journal text fields, the goal, chat, memory, the planner's note of accepted names — unchanged (ISS-0058, ISS-0045 are a later run).

## 10. Instrument of criterion 7 (families, floor, control, budget) — as built from the sketch

- **Families 1–9** as the requirement writes them, **family 10** (section 12 G, found by the sketch), **family 11** (section 12 G, remark G1) and **family 12** (section 12 G, remark V1). Each family is its own `test()` (so a mutant run can select it by name). Each is built as a whole number of repeats of its unit (no cut in the middle) to ≈ 256 KiB, 1 MiB, 4 MiB; a unit ends with a space or a line end so that repeats do not join into a match. Family 11 is the exception on sizes, and families 10 and 12 are one value each, see below.
- **The two outcomes are checked per size, in the same loop as the ratio (G8).** For one family the loop goes over the sizes from small to large; for each size, first `findSecrets(input) === null` and `findSecrets(input + "\n" + bait) !== null` (bait from parts) — a function that stops early or scans only a prefix fails the second — then the timing of that size; after the second size the first ratio is compared, and so on. The test stops at the first failure (an outcome or a ratio). The order "all outcomes for all sizes, then all timings" is not used: on a quadratic code it would run the 4 MiB outcome calls before any ratio is looked at (measured by the gate: family 1 under m5 takes 163 s even in the stopping order).
- **Measure:** the sum of `k` calls timed with `process.hrtime.bigint()` in one process; one warm-up call on the smallest size **before** `k` is chosen (a cold first call otherwise hides the floor: measured, family 11 chose `k = 1` on a cold call); `k` doubled on the smallest size until the sum is ≥ 20 ms (the floor checks the instrument, not the code); the same `k` on the other sizes; one warm-up call per size; **7 repeats, median** (the number the requirement names: the budget does not force a cut, F31, N5); ratios t(1M)/t(256K) and t(4M)/t(1M), both < 8. No absolute time limit on the code.
- **Control of the instrument (G8, V4):** the quadratic sample function (reads the rest of the input for each character, independent of content; family 1's input suffices) with `k = 1`, **one warm-up call on 8 KiB only** (the loop is hot after it; a warm-up on every size cost 28 s of the 58 s), and **3 repeats (median) at each of 8, 32 and 128 KiB**: the number the requirement names for the control. The earlier versions of this design cut the 128 KiB step to one measurement (N5) and called that a use of the requirement's fallback sentence; the gate pass 03f showed that it was not forced (three repeats with eleven families: 94.5 s), and the remark V4 asked for an open choice. **Chosen: three repeats**, because they fit the budget with the twelfth family (F34: the whole instrument 97.4 s of 120 s, margin 1.23) and a design inside the requirement's own numbers needs no amendment of the control (the item G-6 of the earlier version is withdrawn, section 12 G). Conditions: the time at 8 KiB ≥ the floor of 20 ms (else the sizes are raised: an error of the instrument, not a pass); the two-step ratio t(128K)/t(8K) ≥ 64 (measured 201.7 to 214.7, F34, F31); the one-pass sample function on 256 KiB/1 MiB/4 MiB: both ratios < 8 (measured 3.33 to 4.26).
- **Budget (G8, V4):** the test of this criterion ≤ 120 s on the developer machine and ≤ 5 minutes on CI. Measured in the sketch (F34, on a machine where one control call on 8 KiB costs 58 ms, the slower of the two machines seen so far): twelve families at **7 repeats 56.2 s**, the control with three repeats **41.3 s**, together **97.4 s**, so the margin against 120 s is **1.23** (with the single measurement on 128 KiB the whole would take 75.6 s, margin 1.59; the validator measured 94.5 s with eleven families and three repeats, and 71.5 s with the single measurement, on its own machine). The margin is smaller than the 1.8 of the earlier versions, said openly: it is the price of the twelfth family (3.7 s) and of the requirement's own control. The families keep their seven repeats and the control its three; the limits 8, 64 and the floor 20 ms stay. If it does not fit on a machine, the fallback sentence of the requirement applies (families not below five repeats: about 40 s instead of 56 s, the whole about 81 s); this design uses it neither for the families nor for the control. The earlier cuts (five repeats of the families, and one measurement of the control, N5) are withdrawn.
- **Family 10 (section 12 G, finding in F11):** unit `a.`, input `token=` + `a.`×n + `b` (one 4 MiB value that is a path, no secret). It must return «no secret», find the bait at the end, not throw, and keep the ratios < 8. It kills the mutant m28 (a path check with a repeated-group regular expression: `RangeError`).
- **Family 11 (section 12 G, remark G1):** unit `token={{ password=$( Bearer {{ Bearer $( ` (note the final space), repeated, **one line, no line end**: the unclosed `{{` after a name and after `Bearer`, and the unclosed `$(` after a name and after `Bearer`. Each unit is read as kept values (В4 and В2) and has no secret; every unit starts a group search that fails, in the F9 pass (`token={{`, `password=$(`) and in the F8 pass (`Bearer {{`, `Bearer $(`). The unit holds all four so that a mutant that drops the record of one closer in one pass is also caught. Sizes **16 KiB, 64 KiB, 256 KiB** (ratios t(64K)/t(16K) and t(256K)/t(64K) < 8; `k` chosen on 16 KiB): the mutant m31 is quadratic with a large constant, one call of it costs 10 s on 256 KiB and about 160 s on 1 MiB (F22, F23), and the three small sizes keep its run short (the stopping order ends it at the first ratio: 15.1 at 16K → 64K, killed).
