## How to read a scenario

- **The stand.** ORCH starts the app from a fresh clone (`node app/server/node-main.js` on a free port, in-memory data) and
  passes its address; in this file it is `<APP>`. The stand serves the local set of the site (the examples page, the order
  texts and the Kazakh button exist on it). One scenario needs the second stand `<APP-PUBLIC>` (the public set of the open
  build): V-20; if it cannot be started it is "not run: no stand" with the reason, never a skip, and the sign-off names
  criterion 8 as covered only by the tests (LD-9, PB-1, PB-2, PB-6, PB-7) and by the post-deploy record.
- **The browser.** Real Chrome, a fresh temporary profile for every scenario (nothing stored) unless a scenario says
  otherwise, the local server with the headers of REQ-109. The three windows are 1470×784, 390×844 and 320×568; the themes
  are light (the theme button on «Светлая») and dark («Тёмная»); the languages are ru and en. `curl` never replaces a
  browser step.
- **Cells.** A cell is one window × theme × language. FULL = all 12 cells. CORE = six cells: 1470×784 light ru, 1470×784 dark
  en, 390×844 light en, 390×844 dark ru, 320×568 light ru, 320×568 dark en. A scenario that names "+ 360 and 380" also runs
  those two widths (360×800 and 380×800), in both themes and both languages. The runner may run more cells, never fewer.
- **Bad conditions in a real Chrome.** As in REQ-111: the runner holds, replaces or breaks the answer of one request at the
  browser itself (the debugging protocol), emulates "reduce motion", slows the network, switches JavaScript off by the
  browser setting, injects a script at the start of the page, and moves the tab to the background by opening another tab.
  A step "stop the stand and open the page" is never written (AP-125).
- **Names the runner may use.** The status block is `section.info.now` (heading `#now-title`); the three rows are the `li` of
  its list; a mark is `span.stat` (`.on` for «Работает»); the closing paragraph is its last child; the panel is `.idea`; the
  panel area of the result is `.r-side`; the receipt row is `.rc-acc` (pips `.pp i`, count `.pt`, the sentence is its `p`);
  the stage marks are `p.stat-line` inside `#stage-1` to `#stage-4`; the demo area is `#demo`; the scene is `#stage` with
  `data-figure`, its text list is `#stage-text`. These names are in the evidence, never in a phrase for the owner.
- **Console.** Criterion 10 declares the only allowed console lines: the browser's one line for a page answered with 404
  (status and path; `decisions/0118` О2) and, in the failure scenarios, the browser's line of the failed request (once each,
  with its status or error and its address). Any other line is a failure; a warning that no criterion forbids is a remark
  (AP-117). No Content-Security-Policy violation is allowed.
- **Expected and remark.** "Expected" is taken from the criterion verbatim. "Remark" is wider than the criterion (the look,
  the code design, a ruling): a mismatch there goes to the findings as a remark and is never a failure of the scenario
  (AP-117, AP-082).
- **The words.** The ru and en words quoted below are the values of the criterion's tables. A label or a mark may be shown in
  capital letters by the style; compare without letter case, never with a different word.
- **Evidence.** For each step the runner records the observable state it asked for again after the action: the text and the
  state of the page, the value it read (a count, a size, a contrast), and a snapshot. Silence is never a result. Snapshots lie
  outside the project and are listed in the handoff.
- **Severity if it fails.** High - the visitor sees a wrong, missing or false thing; goes to WF-03 in this run and the sign-off
  is FAIL; Medium - blocks as well; Low goes to the findings. Critical, High and Medium block.
- **Kazakh.** kk is built but hidden on production (G9), and its texts are drafts that wait for a native speaker (ISS-0127,
  AP-114). The button «ҚАЗ» exists on the local stand; no scenario presses it. The kk scenarios are one entry, K-01, "not
  run: kk hidden, G9", and are not failures.
