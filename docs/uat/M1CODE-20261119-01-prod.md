## How it was run

- **The browser.** Real Chrome (version 157, headless, the debugging protocol, my own driver in Node 22.23.4), a fresh
  temporary profile in the scratch folder, never the owner's profile; every tab in its own browser context; 1470x784,
  390x844 and 320x568; the theme and the language set by the page's own buttons. Every Chrome started was stopped by its
  own process id.
- **Cells.** To spend few requests a loaded page is switched through its four cells (light ru, light en, dark en, dark
  ru) with the page's buttons and audited in each; the pages were loaded once per window. FULL scenarios were therefore
  run in all 12 cells; CORE scenarios and the browser-side faults in the cells named in each row.
- **The audit of a page state** (own code): the exact words of the test-mode line and of the AI disclosure; the page
  language and the title; no horizontal scroll; no cut text (the nearest block of every visible text, `scrollWidth` not
  above `clientWidth`); the contrast of every visible text (at least 4.5:1) and of the accents (at least 3:1); every
  button and link at least 44x44; the focus ring on every Tab stop (at least 3:1); no Content-Security-Policy violation,
  no exception, no console entry but the declared 404 line; the forbidden-words lint of REQ-111 criterion 3 and the
  ban-list of criterion 10 on the text, `alt`, `title` and `aria-label`; the language buttons exactly RU and EN. For the
  scene: the role, the accessible name, the four desks and the caption, the box inside the window, `overflow-x`, the
  figure height, no cut or overlapping label, the text list, `data-frames` and the picture's hash.
- **Browser-side faults** (L-13, L-22 to L-26, L-28, L-29, L-30, S-09 to S-12) were made at the browser through the
  protocol (a request answered, held or failed in the browser; a script injected at the start of the page; JavaScript
  switched off; reduced motion emulated; a second tab). No production server was stopped or changed.
- **Requests to production.** The logged requests of the browser: 935 (842 `GET` of the site's files and of the
  questionnaire, 93 `POST /api/demo/result`; about 25 of them were answered in the browser by the injected faults and
  never left it) and 38 by `curl` (the headers of five pages, the two demo addresses with the languages, the refusals,
  the files `texts.js` and the health). **The total is above the "few hundred" of the handoff**: a page load is 14
  requests (every file revalidates, `Cache-Control: max-age=0, must-revalidate`), and the matrix the handoff asks for is
  12 cells of four pages and the demo. No load test, no parallel run (one Chrome at a time), about one request per second
  on average, static files and the two demo calls only. I report this to ORCH in `result.issues`; the log of every request
  with its time is in the scratch folder (`<scratch>/prod/res/netlog.txt`).
- **Evidence** (all outside the project, in `<scratch>/prod/`): `res/*.json` (every audit with its numbers), `snaps/`
  (101 snapshots: every page and cell of the matrix, the demo question and result in each cell, the six kinds, the faults),
  `res/curl.txt` and `res/curl2.txt` (the raw answers of the direct requests), `res/netlog.txt`.
