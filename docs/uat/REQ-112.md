# REQ-112 - acceptance scenarios: the stage scene at the top of the public pages (the office in stage mode)

WF-02, step 8, item 1 (BA-ANALYST), run `M1CODE-20261119-01`, 2026-11-21. The requirement is the block REQ-112 in
`docs/requirements.yaml` (10 criteria, as amended by the micro-amendment run `M1AMEND-20261119-01`); the measure of the
drawing is `docs/design/V1-public-look.md` (sections 9 and 10, the step table of 9.13); the code design is
`docs/design/REQ-111-112-public-landing.md` (4.3 and 4.6). The state to accept is HEAD `3c66ba3` of `m0-records`. The
pages that hold the scene, the texts, the themes and the demo are REQ-111: its scenarios are in `docs/uat/REQ-111.md`,
and the rules of its section "How to read a scenario" (the stand `<APP>`, the browser, the cells FULL and CORE, the bad
conditions made at the browser, the names the runner may use, the console rule, "Expected" against "Remark", the

UAT-RUNNER from fresh copies outside the project; this file needs only `<APP>`. They are not repeated here, except where a

**Who acts.** The first-time visitor of the public site: a small-business owner who does not program and does not know
the system. The picture of a small office is the first thing they see, so they must understand it without help and must
never be told something false by it. No real person, event or project is used (AP-004); no scenario spends the owner's
limit or money (no AI run on this site, G2).

**What the scene is, in plain words.** At the top of the landing, of «how it works» and of the demo stands a small pixel
office: a door with the sign «Sai», four desks with the plates «1 · Принято», «2 · Готовим документацию», «3 · Готовим
спецификацию» and «4 · Готово к просмотру», and a figure of the visitor with the label «Вы» standing at the door. Under it
stands the caption «Картинка показывает этапы работы, а не движения людей.» (en «The picture shows work stages, not
people’s movements.»). The roles at the desks are «Руководитель проекта», «Аналитик», «Разработчик» and «Проверяющий»; the
reader of the screen's text list reads them as «1 · Принято — Руководитель проекта» and so on. The figure walks only
after the visitor's own tap (a step of «how it works», an answer of the demo); nothing else in the picture moves.

**Names for this file.** `#stage` reports `data-frames` (the frames drawn since the load) and `data-figure` (the figure's
height in CSS pixels); `#stage-text` is the text list of the scene (four desk lines and one line «Вы у двери» or «Вы у
стола n: <этап>»; en «You are at the door» / «You are at desk n: <stage>»); `#live` is the live region that says «Стол n:
<этап>» (en «Desk n: <stage>») on how.html after a walk. The runner counts frames only through `data-frames`.

**The step table (look 9.13) the scenarios use.** Where «Вы» stands: at load on the landing and on how.html — at the door;
on how.html after a tap on step n — at desk n. On demo.html at load (question 1) — at the door; after the answer to
question 1 — at desk 2; to question 2 — at desk 3; to question 3 — at desk 3 (it is there already: no walk); to question
4 — at desk 4; to question 5 or «Хватит, к результату» — at desk 4 (no walk: it is there already, one frame); «‹ Назад» from
question i — at the desk of question i−1 (at the door for question 1).
