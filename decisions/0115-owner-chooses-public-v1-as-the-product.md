# 0115 — The owner's choice: the finished product is the public site V1 of the program PUBLIC-V1

Status: **accepted** (the owner's words, 2026-11-17; recorded by ORCH; the owner can cancel it).

## Question

On 2026-11-17, after the release of REQ-107 and during the run ISS-0116-20261117-01, the owner wrote: «Продолжи работу, так чтобы я получил готовый продукт». Sai has two possible meanings of "the finished product": the public site V1 of `docs/program/public-v1/` (milestones M0–M7) or the local virtual team on the owner's Mac with real runs; or first the open defects of the e2e tests. ORCH asked the owner which one he means (in the chat, a closed question with three answers).

## Options

1. The public site V1 (the plan `docs/program/public-v1/PLAN.md`, V1 = M0–M7): a closed site on Vercel, accounts by invitation, an AI interview, versioned ТЗ with approval, «В разработку» behind the owner's password, agents build, the office shows the real work; about 45–60 working days of agent work by the plan; the owner's gates (Vercel data, `claude setup-token`, consent for real runs with cost ceilings, and the others of PLAN §3).
2. The local virtual team on the Mac with real runs under the owner's subscription (days; consent for real runs and a ceiling).
3. First the open defects of the e2e tests (ISS-0100…ISS-0117), the product later.

## Decision

The owner chose option 1, in his words (the answer to the question): «Публичный сайт V1 (Recommended)». ORCH finishes the run ISS-0116-20261117-01 to its release, then continues the program PUBLIC-V1 by its plan from the place where it stopped (`docs/program/public-v1/PROGRESS.md`, "Next step"), with the rules of PLAN §0 and §4 and the owner's gates of PLAN §3: what needs the owner (accounts, tokens, consent, money, legal) is asked of him and is never done or guessed by an agent.

## Why

It is the recorded target of Sai since the audit AUD-001 (2026-10-27/28) and the plan written for it; the owner confirmed it as the meaning of "the finished product".

## Boundary

It does not change the quality bar, the gates or the owner's gates of the plan; it does not answer any open owner question (PLAN §3 gates G1–G17, `decisions/0112` О19); the open defects stay in the queue of the plan's lanes.

## Basis

The owner's message of 2026-11-17 «Продолжи работу, так чтобы я получил готовый продукт» and his answer «Публичный сайт V1 (Recommended)» to ORCH's question «Что вы считаете «готовым продуктом» Sai?» (the chat of this ORCH session).
