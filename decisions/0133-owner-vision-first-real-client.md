## Decision

1. **The goal of Sai and its measure of success.** Sai is «working» when the first real client, invited, goes the whole path alone, without the owner: describes the idea → the interview with the AI → the ТЗ → the client approves it → the agents do the work, visible on one page with the tasks, the progress and the questions for the client → the client gets the result. Every later priority is judged by its use for this path (the owner: «главный приоритет»). The text is the owner's own (point 1).
2. **Access.** By invitation only. Open registration waits for two things of the owner: the requirements of the law of the Republic of Kazakhstan on personal data with a lawyer, and the paid API key for real model runs (gate G2 stays as decisions/0127 В says).
3. **The reference product.** The rule of reading it without a trace stays as it is: it is studied as a guide; its code, its design and its closed materials are not copied; its name is not written into Sai. The comparison is about quality, reliability, security, usability and efficiency of the implementation, not about its modules. Ideas and approaches may be taken when they make Sai better and serve its goal. Sai stays its own product; its scope stays (decisions/0099, PLAN §2).
4. **Operating systems.** The owner works with Sai on Windows first and on macOS second. Linux stays on the servers and in GitHub Actions (decisions/0110 stays). **ORCH:** whether the owner can work fully on Windows and macOS without porting the agent engine is for ORCH to find out; until then decisions/0127 Г stands (the engine on the Mac, no port to Windows planned). If a port turns out to be necessary, ORCH brings the owner the reasons, the consequences and the proposed variant before any work on it.
5. **Agents.** The team that builds Sai keeps its roles, workflows and gates; improvements may be proposed when something hinders. Inside the product the agent system is flexible: for each client project it decides which roles, how many agents and how the tasks are shared. **ORCH:** this does not contradict decisions/0127 А: the chain of a development session (interview → ТЗ → development → result) stays fixed; the flexibility is inside the development stage. It becomes a requirement (WF-01) when that stage is specified.
6. **The «second point».** Postponed and unchanged; it is not tied to the API budget; the owner returns to it himself.
7. **When ORCH asks the owner.** Questions that can be solved without a material risk and without changing the goals are solved by the agents. Questions that touch the architecture, the security, the budget, the key functionality, the concept or the deadlines go to the owner with what happened, why it matters, the variants and the recommended one. The gates of push to `main`, deploy, deletion, G21 and G2 stay as written before (decisions/0048, 0130, 0132).
8. **The home page** is already published (https://sai-team.vercel.app); it is not planned again.
9. **ORCH, next.** ORCH checks the order of the stages of decisions/0127 against the goal of item 1 (what the first invited client needs and what can wait) and brings the result to the owner as a plan amendment in PROGRESS.md; the reorder itself is a separate record.

## Why

The owner wants to set the goal and approve the principal decisions, not to manage the technical details («Моя роль — определять общую цель, утверждать принципиальные решения и оценивать конечный результат»). One measurable goal (the first real client alone) lets the agents choose and cut the work themselves. The points that contradicted the records were asked before being written, as the owner asked.

## Boundary

This record does not give G2 or the cap of real runs, does not open registration, does not change the rule of the reference product and does not allow a push to `main`, a deploy or a deletion. It does not reorder the stages by itself (item 9).
